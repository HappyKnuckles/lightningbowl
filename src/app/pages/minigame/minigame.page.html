How to play

Drag sideways to line up, then flick upward to roll. A faster swipe adds power; its angle sets your aim. Set your hook before each delivery. Oil keeps the ball straight until it reaches the dry back end.

Keyboard: focus the lane, use ← / → to move, A / D to aim and [ / ] to change hook. Hold Space to build power, then release to roll. Or use Roll ball for a preset-power delivery.

Ten frames, real bowling scoring: X = strike, / = spare, – = miss. Bonuses appear once your next throws resolve them. Earn bonus balls in frame ten with a strike or spare.

Daily has one saved game per UTC date, with the same lane for everyone. Practice as often as you like. Mini-game scores stay separate from your tracked games.
